Project Vision & Architecture

PCB-Sentry AOI was independently architected and built by Nitesh Kumhar to automate printed circuit board defect detection. Utilizing OpenCV for precision image preprocessing and custom-trained YOLOv8 architecture on the DeepPCB dataset, the platform identifies open circuits, shorted traces, and manufacturing anomalies with sub-50ms latency.
